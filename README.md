### About Me (English)
I am a third-year engineering student with a strong focus on **Embedded Systems, Digital Hardware Design, and Semiconductor Technology**. My work bridges the gap between hardware and software, ranging from register-level C programming on microcontrollers (STM32, ARM Cortex-M, ESP32, Renesas) to RTL logic synthesis and PCB layout. 

* **Currently exploring:** Real-Time Operating Systems (FreeRTOS, Contiki OS), TinyML for edge devices, and FPGA/Digital IC Design.
* **Recent works include:** 
  - Developing firmware for multi-channel DAQ systems (STM32 & 24-bit AD7124 ADC).
  - Building a Python-based SCADA interface over RS485 Modbus RTU for an industrial concrete curing control cabinet.
  - Simulating Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) duty cycles using Contiki OS & Cooja.
* **Goal:** Constantly seeking opportunities to learn, build, and optimize hardware-software integrated systems. Always open to connecting with peers and professionals in the embedded engineering and semiconductor fields.
